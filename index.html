Flashcards

Study one theme at a time. Pick a theme at the top, then tap the card to flip it.

  • Norsk → English / English → Norsk chooses which side you see first.
  • Knew it! marks the word as known; Didn’t know marks it as missed. Both move to the next card. ← Back goes to the previous one.
  • The list next to the buttons filters the cards: all, only the ones you missed, one group of the theme, or one kind of word (en, ei, et, verbs…). Shuffle mixes the order.
  • The coloured label shows the kind of word: en masculine, ei feminine, et neuter, plural, adjective, verb or expression. The note under the translation gives plurals, verb forms, examples and tips.
  • The app remembers the card you stopped at in each theme and in each filter. Cards you already answered under another filter count as done and are skipped, so you don’t see them twice in the same round. The same works in Verbs and Gender.
  • The Full list at the bottom shows every word in the theme.

Practice

All words from every theme in one shuffled deck, with two modes:

  • All cards: a word you mark Knew it! leaves this deck for good. Didn’t know keeps it, and it comes back in the next round. Reset progress puts all words back.
  • Review: only the words that are due today, see below.

Review (spaced repetition)

Every time you answer a card in Flashcards or Practice, the app schedules it for review. Words you know come back less and less often; words you miss come back soon. This is the best way to keep words in long-term memory.

  • Knew it! moves the word one step up. The first time you know a word it goes straight to step 2.
  • Didn’t know sends it back to step 1. In Review it also comes back once more at the end of the same session.
  • Learning are words still being fixed in memory (reviews up to 14 days apart). Mastered are words that reached 30 days or more, which takes several correct reviews spread over a few weeks.
  • Open Practice → Review each day to clear the words that are due. The button shows how many there are.

Verbs

Practise four forms of 500+ verbs. The card shows the infinitive (for example å skrive · to write). Say the present · past · perfect · imperative out loud, then flip to check (skriver · skrev · har skrevet · skriv!).

  • The imperative is the infinitive without the final -e: spise → spis!, komme → kom!. Verbs without -e stay the same (gå!, se!), and after hard clusters the -e stays (sykle!, åpne!). seg becomes deg: sett deg! Verbs that have no imperative, like kunne or synes, show —.
  • Filter by all verbs, the ones you missed, strong verbs (they change the vowel in the past, like skrive → skrev), or one group.
  • The speaker on the back reads all three forms.

Gender

Choose the article for each noun: en, ei or et. You see the noun and its meaning; tap the article (or press 1 2 3).

  • In Bokmål every ei word can also be used with en, so en bok is accepted for ei bok. Other accepted forms mentioned in the card’s note also count as right.
  • Wrong answers are saved; choose Only my mistakes to practise them. Reset progress clears the score, the mistakes and starts a new round.

Clock

Practise telling the time in Norwegian. The card shows a random time; say it out loud, then flip to see how Norwegians say it, for example 04:30 → Klokka er halv fem.

  • Choose Analog (a clock face with hands) or Digital (like 16:30). The app remembers your choice.
  • Times go in steps of five minutes. Norwegians use the 12-hour clock when speaking, so 16:30 is also halv fem.
  • Remember: halv fem is half to five (4:30), and from :20 to :40 you count from the half hour: ti på halv fem = 4:20, fem over halv fem = 4:35.
  • There is no end: a new time comes after each answer. The score shows how many you got right. Reset progress clears it.

Numbers

Practise numbers from 0 to 100. The card shows a random number; say it out loud, then flip to see it written out.

  • Cardinal: 21 → tjueen. Ordinal: 21. → tjueførste. In Norwegian a dot after the number means it is ordinal.
  • Numbers are written as one word: tjuefem, førtiåtte. Some have another accepted form, shown under the word (sju / syv).
  • Like Clock, there is no end: a new number comes after each answer. Each mode keeps its own score; Reset progress clears the score of the mode you are in.

Search

Find any word in all themes. Type a whole word or part of one, choose its language (NO or EN) and press the magnifier.

  • You can type without special letters: vaeske finds væske, o finds ø, a finds å.
  • Under each result is its theme and group. Tap it to open that card in Flashcards.

Progress

  • Cards today: answers today, your daily goal and your streak.
  • Words known: words you marked Knew it! in Flashcards or Practice.
  • Due for review today, with a button to start the review.
  • Learning / mastered: see Review above.
  • Right in Gender and verb forms known.
  • By theme: progress in each theme. Tap a theme to open it.

Daily goal and streak

  • Every answer in Flashcards, Practice, Verbs and Gender counts towards today’s goal. It is shown under the app name in the menu.
  • Choose the goal in Settings: 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100 cards a day. It starts at 20.
  • Your streak counts the days in a row on which you reached the goal. If you haven’t reached it yet today, yesterday’s streak still counts until the day is over.

NO / EN names

The NO | EN switch at the top right of every page changes the language of the names in the app:

  • the word Tema / Theme next to the theme list;
  • the theme names, for example Kroppen / The body;
  • the sections of the theme list, for example Hverdagsliv / Everyday life;
  • the groups of words inside a theme, for example daglige rutiner / Daily routine.

The words on the cards don’t change. The app remembers your choice; it starts in NO.

Pronunciation

Tap the speaker on a card to hear the word. In Settings you can choose the voice and test it.

If no Norwegian voice is listed, install one on your device. Android: Settings → Text-to-speech → Google → Install voice data → Norwegian. iPhone/iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Norwegian. Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → Add voices → Norsk.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Space or Enter: flip the card
  • → Knew it! · ← Didn’t know · Backspace Back
  • Gender: 1 en · 2 ei · 3 et · Enter next
  • Esc closes the menu on small screens

Settings

Open Settings in the menu to change:

  • Appearance: Automatic follows your device’s light or dark mode; Light or Dark keeps it fixed.
  • Daily goal: 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100 cards a day, see Daily goal.
  • Pronunciation: the voice that reads the words, with a Test button, see Pronunciation.
  • Reset progress: deletes everything saved (progress, reviews, goal, streak and settings), after you confirm. The app starts again as for a new user. The Reset progress buttons in Flashcards, Practice, Verbs and Gender only clear that page.

Reset progress

There are five Reset progress buttons. Four of them clear only their own page; the one in Settings clears everything.

WhereWhat it deletes
FlashcardsThe words you knew and missed in the open theme only. The theme starts again from the first card.
PracticeThe words you marked Knew it! and the ones you missed. All words come back into the deck.
VerbsThe verbs you knew and missed. The verb deck starts again.
GenderThe score and your saved mistakes. A new round starts.
SettingsEverything: all of the above, Review, daily goal, streak and settings. Asks you to confirm first.
  • The four page buttons don’t touch Review, your daily goal and streak, or the other pages and themes.
  • They delete at once, with no confirmation. Only the button in Settings asks first.
  • Nothing can be undone. Progress on your other devices is not affected.

Where your progress is saved

Your progress is saved in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account.

  • Your phone and your computer each have their own progress. The installed app and the browser on the same phone share it.
  • Clearing the browser’s data for this site deletes your progress.

Install the app

  • Android / computer (Chrome, Edge): tap Install app at the bottom of the menu, or use the browser menu ⋮ → Install.
  • iPhone / iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Firefox and Safari on Mac don’t offer the same install. There, Install app shows the steps for your browser.
  • Once installed, the app also works without internet.

About this app

Norsk Flashcards was made by Marcus Lima (LinkedIn) for his own study of Norwegian, working towards level A2–B1. The code was written with Claude, Anthropic’s AI assistant; the features, the choice of words, the review of the content and the testing were done by the author.

It is a single HTML file with plain JavaScript, no framework and no server: progress is stored only in your browser, pronunciation uses your device’s voices, and it works offline as an installable app.

Source code, technical details and how it was built: github.com/mvclima/norsk-flashcards · Open source under the MIT license.
